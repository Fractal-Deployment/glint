## What GLINT is, in the operator's framing (2026-11-04)
- Goal: BF16 or FP32 fidelity at approximately the size of a 4-bit model; not running it as 4-bit but at the size of 4-bit. Precision is a per-model choice (INT8, BF16, TF32, 4-bit).
- Three separate mechanisms: folding (from NF4's unfold-as-needed, with GLINT's own version), streaming (stream in what was lost, so there is a fidelity model; long-term), and plugs (still necessary). Then combinations, and finally all three active at once.
- End picture: a roughly 20B model split between 32 GB of CPU RAM and the GPU, where the model's reaction to the tokens determines unfolding and prefetch, then streaming fills in some weights and plugs fill in later parts; slower than NF4 but conversational. Start with a very small model; the point is that the mechanisms work together.
- Underlying premise: the model does not need all of itself to start fulfilling the prompt.
- Concept document (operator-endorsed): fold/compress -> unfold -> conditional relational refinement under a moving VRAM budget; the plug is refinement information, not definitionally "the remaining bits".

## Words that mean several things on disk (name which one)
- **Plug**: (1) the GLINT child nibble, 4 bits inside the NF4 cell the hole selects (`glint_pin_v1`); (2) the Keystone bitplane plug (bit-exact BF16); (3) the nested-NF8 sub-index (old, Oct 21); (4) the `p7_plug_res4_v1` residual of the INT8 base against BF16 (`tools/plug_encode.c`). The ledger's "plug (res4)" rows are kind 4.
- **Resolution**: (a) Mode 5 as planned: a weight-residual map streamed back during unfold; (b) unmerged glint #34/#70: the weight residual in pinned RAM with DecDEC-style channel selection; (c) merged on main: an activation-quantization residual second pass with no extra file or stream. The ledger and the Mojo forward use (c).
- **Modes**: the Oct-20 plan/round-3 rules/GitHub issues: M1 plain INT8, M2 INT8 + CPU offload, M3 fold with plugs in RAM, M4 fold + compression (lossless/lossy codec), M5 resolution. A Gemini proposal in REPORT-2026-10-21 reads M4 as selective layer unfolding and M5 as activation-guided unfold; that is not the mode list. The five modes are a paraphrase, not the operator's verbatim words. A six-mode ladder (Mode 6 = policy) was pasted Nov 2.
- **Hole**: the 4-bit parent cell. Measured 2026-11-04: the hole alone is exactly NF4 (fold-only PPL 5.833352, top-1 1864/2047, identical to the NF4 arm).

## Decode (added later the same day; PRs #88, #90)
A KV-cache decode path now exists (`ARM0_DECODE`), validated against prefill and the padded-forward rollout. Decode compute per layer: dense bf16 cuBLAS 0.97 ms (32 tokens/s); the old GLINT kernel (H-TILE) 14.2 ms (2.2 tokens/s); the new GLINT decode GEMV 1.24 ms hole + plug (25.3 tokens/s) and 1.18 ms fold only (26.6 tokens/s), about ten times more accurate than H-TILE against a double-precision reference. With the whole plug streamed through the ring in decode: 4.62 ms per layer, 6.8 tokens/s, transfer-bound, output identical to resident. VRAM: all-streamed plug saves 1305 MiB. Fetching a selected fraction of the plug from a K-major layout: 5% costs 0.31-0.35 ms per layer, below the layer compute. Rollout: no activation-selected arm (10-25% of the plug) tracks the full model past 6-7 generated tokens (3 prompts; act vs random not separable).
